I've been looking forward to reading this for a while and it finally made it
up my backlog stack. Most of the topics and opinions in the book I was already
familiar with and had talked with Mathias about. Nonetheless this was an
extremely easy, actionable, and good book to read. It was really nice to have
this kind of handbook to read through a lot of the aspects of leadership. And
since the book is also structured as a kind of workbook with helpful prompts
at the end of every chapter, it is immediately very actionable as well. I can
highly recommend it for anyone who is or aspires to be in any kind of
leadership or management position.
