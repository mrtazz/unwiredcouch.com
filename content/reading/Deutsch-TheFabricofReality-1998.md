I was curious to read more about the work of David Deutsch ever since reading
[The Science of Can and Can't](/reading/marletto-thescienceofcanandcant-2021/)
and the general topic of reality is one I find interesting even though a lot
of things in that field go way over my head. And this book was no different.
I overall enjoyed it although I can't claim to have been able to follow every
line of argument thoroughly. And I'm not sure I fully grasped how his four
strands of reality exactly come together. But nonetheless there was tons of
interesting and thought provoking content even in the chapters alone.
