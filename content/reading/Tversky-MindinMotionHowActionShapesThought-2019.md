I had this on my shelf for a long time and finally picked it up. And I very
much enjoyed the book. I took a lot of notes and the connection between mind
and motion, thinking and action, and how it shapes thinking about it all is
super interesting to me. Overall I didn't really get a strong sense of a kind
of trajectory I was on while reading the book. It felt more to me like a
collection of really interesting areas to think about. So I would've enjoyed
having a kind of sense of where to go from there. But nonetheless it was great
to read and gave me a lot to think about.
