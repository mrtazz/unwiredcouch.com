As I mentioned before after reading
[[30_zk/30.02_refs/Arden-TheBearandTheNightingale-2017/Arden-TheBearandTheNightingale-2017.md]]
I immediately ordered the follow up books from the series. And this second one
was even more exciting and fun than the first one. I was immediately hooked
when I started reading. And wanted to kinda read the third one right away but
convinced something else first to savor the joyful anticipation some more.

This book also has a wonderful quote that fits so much in our current time:

> "Why carve things of wood [..] if you can make marvelous things of ice with
> only your hands?"
>
> "I carve things of wood because things made by effort are more real than
> things made by wishing"

This interaction between Morozko (Father Frost) and Vasya the protagonist
feels so relevant to these current times of generative AI and I was very happy
to find it in this book.
