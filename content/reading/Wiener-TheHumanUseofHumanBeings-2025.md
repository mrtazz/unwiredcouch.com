I came across this book when I read [Why Machines
Learn](/reading/ananthaswamy-whymachineslearn-2025/) and have been curious
since what a book written in the 1950s has to say about machines and humans
and their interactions. The book overall talks about a lot more than just that
though. It reads like a bigger collection of philosophical and scientific
ideas about the world and is very interesting in this. There are numerous
passages that feel like they could've been written today as they are still as
relevant as they were 75 years ago.
