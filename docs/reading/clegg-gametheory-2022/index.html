  • Game Theory 📚
  • by Brian Clegg
  • Publication date: 2022-01-01
  • Read: May 17, 2026

I didn’t really know what to make of this book when I bought it other than that I wanted to get a refresher on game theory and maybe see what a more modern perspective on it could look like. And I think the book definitely delivered on that. A lot of the basics weren’t new to me but fairly good refreshers. And the book brings in more modern topics like the auction of mobile bandwidths spectrums to illustrate how game theory can be utilised to guide the process to have certain constraints. It’s a quick read and I can really recommend it if you aren’t already super familiar with game theory.
